How it works
  • Open any PDF (link, address bar, or local file) — it lands here instead of Chrome's viewer.
  • The PDF docks to the left or right (swap with Alt+S); the free space is your notebook.
  • Notes are tied to the page you're reading and switch as you scroll. Pin to hold a page.
  • Select text in the PDF → Add to notes quotes it into that page's note.
  • Got a pen tablet or stylus? Switch the notes pane to Draw for a per-page sketch pad, or press A to write directly on the PDF pages. Pressure, palm rejection, undo, eraser.
  • Notes are keyed to the document's fingerprint, so they survive renames and moves. Export as Markdown any time.
